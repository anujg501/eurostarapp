Eurostar · Bracelet Photo Bulk Upload

Upload all your bracelet photos in one go — they go live on the storefront.

Checking your admin session…
How to use: 1) Make sure you are logged into the Eurostar Admin in another tab. 2) Pick a style below. 3) Click “Select all photos for this style” and choose that style’s folder of images (files are named by colour, e.g. red.jpg) — they auto-match to the right colour and upload. You can also upload/replace any single colour with its own Upload link.
